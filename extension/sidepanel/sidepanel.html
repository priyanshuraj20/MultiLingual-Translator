Extension Setup Guide
1 Extract the downloaded **Voxa.zip** folder onto your computer.
2 Open browser and visit **chrome://extensions/** settings page.
3 Enable **Developer Mode** toggle in the top-right corner.
4 Click **"Load unpacked"** and select the unzipped **Voxa** folder.
5 Press **`Ctrl + Shift + U`** (or right-click 'Activate Voxa Capture') on your meeting tab to activate capture.
